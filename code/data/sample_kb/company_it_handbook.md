# NovaRetail Internal IT Support Handbook

> Sample knowledge-base content for this project. Replace it with approved company guidance before using the assistant for real support.

## VPN Access
Employees working outside the office must use the company VPN before accessing internal systems. Install the NovaSecure VPN client from the internal Software Portal. Sign in with your company email and password, complete MFA, and select the closest corporate gateway. If the connection fails, first confirm internet connectivity, restart the client, and try another corporate gateway. Do not disable endpoint security or firewall settings to make the VPN work. If the problem remains, open an IT ticket with a screenshot of the error.

## Password Reset Policy
Company passwords must be at least 14 characters. Employees should use the self-service identity portal for normal password resets. After five failed login attempts, the account may be temporarily locked. If self-service recovery fails, contact IT Service Desk. Employees must never share passwords or MFA codes with support personnel.

The portal's web address and the identity checks it uses are not specified in this sample handbook. Open it from a company-approved bookmark or intranet page; do not guess a URL or follow an unsolicited reset link. If you cannot find the approved portal or cannot complete recovery, contact the IT Service Desk through a known company channel. Do not send a password, one-time code, recovery code, or MFA approval to support.

If you are unsure whether a password change completed, use the normal company sign-in page to check. Avoid repeated guesses: five failed login attempts may temporarily lock the account. If the account is locked or the new password is not accepted, stop trying and contact the IT Service Desk.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for email, VPN, cloud administration, and other protected company systems. The approved methods are the company authenticator app and registered hardware security keys. SMS should only be used when explicitly enabled as a recovery method.

Approve an MFA request only when you initiated the sign-in and recognize the application or action. If you receive an unexpected prompt, deny it and report it to the IT Service Desk. If your registered device is lost, replaced, or unavailable, use the identity portal's approved recovery flow if available; otherwise contact the IT Service Desk for identity verification and re-enrollment instructions. Never share an MFA code or approve a prompt at someone else's request.

## Software Installation
Employees should install business software only from the company Software Portal. Requests for software not listed in the portal require manager approval and IT security review. Administrator access is not granted simply to install unapproved applications.

## Access to Company Resources
Use your own company account and request only the access needed for your role. If a shared drive, application, or mailbox is missing, first confirm that you signed in with the intended company account and that the resource is available to your team. Request access through the approved company process and include the resource name and the business reason. Do not use another employee's account or ask them to share credentials. The approval workflow and access-request URL are not specified in this sample handbook; ask the IT Service Desk or your manager for the approved route.

## Lost or Stolen Laptop
Immediately report a lost or stolen company laptop to the IT Service Desk and your manager. Provide the device asset tag if available and the last known location. IT will initiate device lock/wipe procedures and coordinate with Security. Do not attempt to remotely recover the device yourself.
